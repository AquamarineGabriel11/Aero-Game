# ONÇA — letra final

**TRIP · Track 02 · 🇧🇷 Río · Iguazú · Amazonas · baile funk + guitarra de bossa · vibra 2024 (verde lima, animal print)**

```text
ONÇA
TRIP · Track 02

[Intro]
(a violão, a paddle hitting the water on the beat, then a baile funk drum kicks in)
Rema, rema…
onça…

[Verse 1]
Landed in Rio, Cristo's arms open wide,
you opened yours the same and pulled me to your side,
barefoot on the sand, seventeen on your back,
dribbling like Neymar — golaço, Lima! — run it back.
Iguaçu was roaring right above our heads,
too loud to say "I love you," so you kissed me instead.

[Pre-Chorus]
Dressed to impress for a canoe — I know, I know, it's a lot,
jaguar print on my bikini, lip gloss, sweat, the whole plot,
your three-reais compass frozen dead between the N and S —
"it's broken." "No… we're the middle of the world." And I say yes…

[Chorus]
Onça, onça (rema, rema),
jaguar print and a canoe for two,
onça, onça (rema, rema),
só pra frente, só pra frente with you,
lime-green summer, no signal, no crowd,
rema, rema — say it out loud.

[Post-Chorus]
Rema, rema, rema, rema (só pra frente),
rema, rema, rema, rema (meu amor).

[Verse 2]
A dolphin breaks the indigo, gone before I scream,
a toucan judging my outfit like a mean girl in a dream,
an anaconda slides by slow, you don't even blink,
you call every caiman an alligator — you're cuter than you think,
Dubai chocolate melting, pistachio on my thumb,
and somewhere in the trees, a jaguar waiting for the drum.

[Pre-Chorus]
Dressed to impress for a canoe — I know, I know, it's a lot,
jaguar print on my bikini, lip gloss, sweat, the whole plot,
your three-reais compass frozen dead between the N and S —
"it's broken." "No… we're the middle of the world." And I say yes…

[Chorus]
Onça, onça (rema, rema),
jaguar print and a canoe for two,
onça, onça (rema, rema),
só pra frente, só pra frente with you,
lime-green summer, no signal, no crowd,
rema, rema — say it out loud.

[Post-Chorus]
Rema, rema, rema, rema (só pra frente),
rema, rema, rema, rema (meu amor).

[Verse 3]
Then the sky breaks open, the whole jungle pouring down,
rowing through a cachoeira, soaked and laughing loud,
tacacá so hot, the jambu makes my mouth go numb —
or maybe that was you, I can't tell where it's coming from,
strawberries dipped in chocolate, violão soaking wet,
and you just keep on playing — best summer yet.

[Bridge]
(the beat drops: crickets, the violão alone, a jaguar purring somewhere far)
Then the night comes all at once,
fireflies blinking like the jungle's texting back,
two hammocks, side by side,
you whisper, "ninguém vai nos achar aqui,"
no looking back, no rearview…
just the river… and you.

[Final Chorus]
(sunrise: the baile funk drum slams back in, everyone singing)
Onça, onça (rema, rema),
jaguar print and a canoe for two,
onça, onça (rema, rema),
só pra frente, só pra frente with you,
lime-green summer, no signal, no crowd,
rema, rema — say it out loud.

[Post-Chorus]
Rema, rema, rema, rema (só pra frente),
rema, rema, rema, rema (meu amor).

[Outro]
(the drum fades, the violão and the paddle keep going)
Obrigada, Lima…
rema, rema…
só pra frente…
```

## Traducción al español (para entender)

```text
[Intro]
Rema, rema… / jaguar…

[Verso 1]
Aterrizamos en Río, el Cristo con los brazos abiertos,
tú abriste los tuyos igual y me jalaste a tu lado,
descalzo en la arena, el diecisiete en tu espalda,
driblando como Neymar — ¡golazo, Lima! — otra vez.
Iguazú rugía justo sobre nuestras cabezas,
tan fuerte que no se oía un "te quiero", así que mejor me besaste.

[Pre-Coro]
Me arreglé para impresionar… para subirme a una canoa. Ya sé, ya sé, es mucho,
bikini de jaguar, brillo de labios, sudor, todo el show,
tu brújula de tres reales congelada entre la N y la S:
"está rota." "No… somos el centro del mundo." Y yo digo que sí…

[Coro]
Jaguar, jaguar (rema, rema),
animal print y una canoa para dos,
jaguar, jaguar (rema, rema),
solo hacia adelante, solo hacia adelante contigo,
verano verde lima, sin señal, sin gente,
rema, rema, dilo fuerte.

[Post-Coro]
Rema, rema, rema, rema (solo hacia adelante),
rema, rema, rema, rema (mi amor).

[Verso 2]
Un delfín rompe el agua índigo y se va antes de que grite,
un tucán juzga mi outfit como la chica mala en un sueño,
una anaconda pasa despacio, y tú ni parpadeas,
a cada caimán le dices alligator; eres más lindo de lo que crees,
chocolate de Dubái derritiéndose, pistache en mi dedo,
y en algún lugar entre los árboles, un jaguar esperando el ritmo.

[Verso 3]
Luego el cielo se abre, toda la selva cae a cántaros,
remamos debajo de una cascada, empapados, riéndonos fuerte,
el tacacá tan caliente que el jambu me duerme la boca…
o tal vez fuiste tú, ya no sé de dónde viene,
fresas bañadas en chocolate, la guitarra empapada,
y tú sigues tocando: el mejor verano de todos.

[Puente]
Luego la noche cae de golpe,
luciérnagas parpadeando como si la selva me contestara los mensajes,
dos hamacas, lado a lado,
me susurras: "nadie nos va a encontrar aquí",
sin mirar atrás, sin retrovisor…
solo el río… y tú.

[Outro]
Gracias, Lima… / rema, rema… / solo hacia adelante…
```
